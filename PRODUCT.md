# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Fahh is for people who use meme sounds socially: friend groups, students, casual creators, prank and reaction-video makers, and anyone who wants a funny audio response immediately. They often use the app one-handed, in a noisy room, under time pressure, and on a wide range of Android devices. Their main job is to choose a sound, trigger it at exactly the right moment, optionally record the reaction, and share the clip with minimal setup.

## Product Purpose

Fahh is the fastest, most satisfying way to play a meme reaction sound while recording video. Its advantage over an ordinary soundboard is that the sound and live reaction are captured together, ready to trim and share. Success means more useful sound choices and repeat creation without turning the core experience into an ad trap, account funnel, or complicated editor.

## Brand Personality

Playful, physical, generous. The voice is self-aware, warm, concise, and a little chaotic. The app should feel like an indie creator made a genuinely fun toy, not like a casino, a generic premium utility, or a growth funnel wearing meme language.

## Anti-references

- Crowded soundboard grids with tiny labels and unclear tap targets.
- Forced sign-up before the first laugh or first recording.
- Fake countdowns, false “viral” claims, misleading price anchors, or hidden renewal terms.
- Aggressive interstitial ads that interrupt recording, preview, export, or rapid button presses.
- Overdecorated glass panels, excessive gradients, and visual effects that compete with the red button.
- Gamification that pressures users to return rather than helping them create or share something funny.

## Design Principles

1. The laugh is one tap away. Preserve instant sound playback and keep the main red button as the hero.
2. Monetization follows delight. Let users experience value before showing an unlock or purchase choice.
3. Free stays genuinely useful. Core playback, camera, editing, sharing, and a useful set of sounds remain available without payment or an account.
4. Free means complete. Core playback, recording, editing, sharing, and sound discovery must remain financially accessible; optional rewarded unlocks may add convenience without requiring payment.
5. Privacy and trust are product features. Keep video and custom audio local by default, ask only for necessary permissions, and explain any data collection plainly.
6. Earned familiarity beats novelty. Use standard Android navigation, billing, search, filtering, and accessibility patterns; reserve personality for copy, sound, physical feedback, and celebrations.
7. Let shared clips carry the name. New reaction videos include a small, unobtrusive Fahh watermark for attribution. Keep it away from faces and captions, and never let a branding/export failure block or destroy the user's recording.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast where applicable, 48 dp minimum touch targets, meaningful screen-reader semantics, scalable text, reduced-motion behavior, visible focus and state changes, and layouts that remain usable on narrow phones and with larger font settings. Do not rely on color, sound, haptics, or motion alone to communicate status. Keep the content rating and store disclosures accurate for every included sound.
